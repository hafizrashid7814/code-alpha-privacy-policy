This policy explains how the game "code alpha" (ECHO: Last Shipment) handles information.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (for example your level progress and aim sensitivity) are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us.

Analytics and diagnostics

The game is built with Unity. Unity may automatically collect anonymous technical and usage information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash and performance diagnostics, and an anonymous identifier. This is used to understand how the game performs and to fix problems. See Unity's privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Permissions

The game uses the INTERNET permission, which is needed for Unity's analytics and diagnostics services. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

Advertising and third parties

The game does not show ads. We do not sell your data. The only third-party service involved is Unity, as described above.

Children

The game contains stylised shooting and violence and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
